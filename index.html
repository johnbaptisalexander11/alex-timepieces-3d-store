Questions

Is this a real shop?

No. Alex Timepieces is a practice brand for practising 3D e-commerce pages. Prices and specifications are examples and nothing is for sale.

How do I change the colour?

Use the arrows on the first screen, swipe left or right there, or tap a colour dot on the overview screen.

Can I rotate the watch?

Yes. On the detail screens, drag sideways anywhere to spin it. Scrolling also turns it to show the strap, the pushers and the caseback.

How are the colours made?

All six watches are the same 3D model. The page recolours the bezel, strap, pushers and hands in code, so adding a colourway is a few lines.
